This policy explains what StudLit ("we", "us") collects when you use the StudLit web app, why we collect it, and who else sees it. We've tried to write it in plain language rather than boilerplate.

Who runs StudLit

StudLit is operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], based in [COUNTRY]. You can reach us at [CONTACT EMAIL] with any question about this policy or your data.

What we collect

Content you give us

When you generate study material, we receive whatever you submit: the topic you type, the text of documents you upload (PDF or Word), images you upload, and any URLs you paste. We also receive essays you submit to the Paper Grader and messages you send to the AI tutor.

Information stored on your own device

Your saved study sets, XP, streaks, badges, theme, and preferences are stored in your browser's local storage — on your device, not on our servers. We cannot see them, and we cannot recover them for you. Clearing your browser data deletes them permanently.

Technical information

Our hosting provider records standard server logs, including your IP address, for security and abuse prevention. We use your IP address in memory to enforce rate limits so that one person cannot exhaust the service for everyone. We do not build advertising profiles and we do not sell data.

Who else sees your content

OpenAI

Everything you submit for AI processing is sent to OpenAI, which generates the response. This includes uploaded documents, images, essays, and chat messages. Their handling of that data is governed by OpenAI's own policies. If your material is confidential, or belongs to someone else, do not upload it.

Netlify

Netlify hosts the site and runs our server functions. Generated results are held in Netlify's storage for two hours so that long generations can finish in the background and be delivered to your browser, then they expire automatically.

Stripe

If and when paid plans are enabled, card payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.

How long we keep things

  • Generation results: two hours in temporary storage, then deleted automatically.
  • Uploaded files: held only for the duration of the request. We do not keep a copy after processing.
  • Your study sets and stats: on your device, for as long as you keep them.
  • Server logs: retained by our hosting provider under their standard retention period.

Age requirement

StudLit is intended for users aged 13 and over. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the minimum age is 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children below these ages. If you believe a child has used StudLit, contact us and we will delete what we hold.

Your choices

Because most of your data lives on your own device, you have direct control over it: clearing your browser storage removes your study sets, stats, and preferences immediately. For anything held on our side, or in server logs, email us at [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will delete what we can identify as yours.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, export, or erase your personal data, and to complain to a data protection authority.

Cookies

StudLit does not use advertising or tracking cookies. We use browser local storage to remember your settings and saved work, which is not shared with anyone.

Security

We use HTTPS everywhere and keep API credentials on the server, never in the browser. No service is perfectly secure, so please don't upload material you cannot afford to have exposed.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use StudLit after a change means you accept the updated policy.
